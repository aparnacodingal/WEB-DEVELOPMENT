Japanese cuisine

Japanese cuisine, known traditionally as washoku, focuses on fresh, seasonal ingredients, delicate flavors, and artistic presentation. Meals are usually built around rice, miso soup, and side dishes like grilled fish or pickled vegetables. Globally famous staples include sushi (vinegared rice paired with seafood), tempura (lightly battered and deep-fried seafood or vegetables), and diverse noodle varieties like ramen, udon, and soba.
